Warriors at Clippers FAQ

When is Warriors at Clippers?

The game is scheduled for Wednesday, April 15, 2026, at 10:00 PM ET.

Where is the game being played?

The game is scheduled at Intuit Dome in Inglewood, California.

What broadcast is listed?

The official NBA schedule listing checked on April 13, 2026, shows Prime Video.

What seed are the teams?

The Clippers are No. 9 in the West and the Warriors are No. 10.

Does the winner make the playoffs?

No. The winner advances to another Play-In game for the No. 8 seed. The loser is eliminated.

Why does the page keep mentioning April 12?

Because the Clippers beat the Warriors 115-110 on April 12, and that result directly created this rematch.

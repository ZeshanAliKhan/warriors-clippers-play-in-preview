What the result means

This is the 9-vs-10 Play-In game in the West. The simplest way to read that bracket slot is this: the loser is eliminated immediately, while the winner advances to another Play-In game for the No. 8 seed.

That is why this page should not be treated like a normal watch guide. The time and TV line matter, but the format matters just as much.

Why the venue matters

Because the Clippers earned the No. 9 slot, they host the game at Intuit Dome. That keeps the rematch in the same building where the Clippers beat Golden State 115-110 on April 12 to set up this bracket result.
